# Abstention and escalation

Abstain from creating an agenda topic only when the current state lacks
traversable source evidence or falls after the meeting date. Report the reason.
Do not invent an owner, impact, due date, or requested decision.

An abstention from agenda topics does not erase evidence-backed confirmed
decisions, completed work, or open work. Return those collections as context.

Keep an owner gap visible. The human chair may assign it, combine the topic, or
remove it from the draft.

If source revisions conflict and no source is authoritative, do not invent a
resolution. Surface the conflict with its evidence references and request a
fact owner or a management ruling, as appropriate.
