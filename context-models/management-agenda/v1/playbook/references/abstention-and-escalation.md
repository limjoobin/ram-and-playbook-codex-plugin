# Abstention and escalation

Abstain from creating an agenda item when the evidence does not establish a
current material state, a management question, or a defensible owner. Report a
warning and state what evidence or ownership confirmation is missing.

Escalate an owner gap only when assigning or confirming ownership is itself a
material management decision. Otherwise defer the candidate for normal
operational follow-up.

If source revisions conflict and no source is authoritative, do not invent a
resolution. Surface the conflict with its evidence references and request a
fact owner or a management ruling, as appropriate.
