# Management review charter and audience

This forum is a management review for senior functional leaders. It establishes
a concise shared baseline of confirmed decisions, recent completed work, open
work, risks, dependencies, approvals, and unresolved conflicts. The intended
audience is accountable leaders and named owners.

An agenda topic may support a progress check, unblock, approval, risk review,
conflict resolution, or decision. It does not need to arrive with a separately
authored management question or impact statement.

Prefer the smallest number of consequential items that fit the meeting. A
well-supported quiet issue can be more appropriate than a high-volume update.
Keep the complete context registers separate from the timeboxed topic list.
Use the compact owner-grouped action register for the status portion and let the
human chair trim or combine the generated topics.
