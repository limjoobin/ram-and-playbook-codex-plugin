# Management review charter and audience

This forum is a decision-focused review for senior functional leaders. It
establishes a concise shared baseline of confirmed decisions, recent completed
work, and open work before resolving material cross-team risks, approvals,
trade-offs, and commitments that cannot be responsibly closed in routine team
status meetings.

An agenda item must help the group make, confirm, or explicitly defer a
management-level decision. The intended audience is accountable leaders and
named owners, not a general operational-update audience.

Prefer the smallest number of consequential items that fit the meeting. A
well-supported quiet issue can be more appropriate than a high-volume update.
Keep informational context separate from items that require a new management
decision. Use a compact owner-grouped action register for the status portion;
do not turn every action into its own timeboxed decision item.
