# Prepare a management review agenda

Use the linked materials as the sole business-policy source for this task:

- [Meeting charter and audience](references/meeting-charter.md)
- [Agenda qualification rules](references/agenda-qualification-rules.md)
- [Agenda-item output schema](references/agenda-item-output-schema.md)

Retrieve evidence before ranking candidates. Apply the lifecycle rules first,
then the qualification rules. Do not turn a routine update into a management
item. Emit only the output fields in the schema, cite every material assertion,
and use the abstention rules whenever the available evidence cannot support a
decision-ready item.
